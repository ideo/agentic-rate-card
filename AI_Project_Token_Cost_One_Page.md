*◉ POWER GUIDE*

• *●●○○○ Fast + economical* — clear, repeatable tasks · GPT-5.6 Luna / Claude Haiku 4.5  
• *●●●●○ Strong + versatile* — synthesis, judgment, and most professional work · GPT-5.6 Terra / Claude Sonnet 5  
• *●●●●● Maximum reasoning* — ambiguity, deep work, coding, and autonomous projects · GPT-5.6 Sol / Claude Opus 5  

*◉ ADDING MORE AI WORKERS*

An **agent** is an AI worker that can take a goal, use tools, and complete a workstream. Parallel agents help when each has a distinct role—such as researcher, builder, critic, or fact-checker.

```text

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

8 agents             3–12h           ~$158              ~$145
```

*HOW TO READ THIS*

- **Input** is everything the AI reads. **Output** is everything it writes or reasons through.
- **K** means thousand tokens; **M** means million tokens. A token is a small piece of text or data.
- **Time** is elapsed wall-clock time, not human labor saved.
- **Costs** are raw, standard API-equivalent model charges—not ChatGPT or Claude subscription prices.
- Search, connectors, image generation, hosting, and human time are not included.

> **Accuracy note:** published model prices and the cost arithmetic are current as of **August 12, 2026**. Task token bands, elapsed times, power scores, and agent multipliers are informed planning estimates—not vendor benchmarks, guarantees, or SLAs. Overnight runs need a clear scope, spending limit, checkpoints, and morning human review.
